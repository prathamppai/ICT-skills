## 💡 Pro Tips

1. **SVGs > Photos** for diagrams — crisp, tiny, editable
2. **Live demos replace slides**: gestures, file creation, search, email
3. **Physical props** (phone, cables) = zero slide time for ports/gestures
4. **Reuse visuals**: `ipo-cycle.svg` ×2, `internet-email.svg` ×3, `hero.jpg` ×2
5. **Speaker notes = your textbook** — slides stay clean, you deliver depth
